## What Is BetterDisplay for Mac

betterdisplay for mac (formerly BetterDummy) is a display management utility for macOS that unlocks your
screens: it converts displays into fully scalable HiDPI panels, controls brightness, volume and color through
software and DDC, creates virtual dummy displays, and offers XDR/HDR upscaling — all from the menu bar. The
question most people ask is how it compares to the tools they already have, so this guide sets betterdisplay
mac side by side with the built-in options and the single-purpose apps it can replace.

## Where BetterDisplay Pulls Ahead

The clearest gap is resolution. Where macOS shows a short list of scaled presets, download betterdisplay for
mac lets you create custom HiDPI modes manually and redefine certain system display parameters — turning a
blurry external monitor into a crisp, Retina-quality workspace. It also handles displays with a notch, HDR,
HDCP or high refresh rate, and on Apple Silicon it can produce scaled HiDPI resolutions now even without
mirroring a dummy, something the built-in settings simply can't do.

## More Than a Brightness App

A standalone brightness app might match DDC control, but betterdisplay mac download folds far more into one
place. Beyond adjusting brightness, volume and color via sliders and keyboard shortcuts, it creates virtual
dummy displays for headless Macs and servers, builds Picture in Picture windows of any real or dummy display,
and streams a display's contents to another screen — including off-center streaming to use the bottom half of
a big TV as a wide workspace. Consolidating all of that is what makes custom resolution mac worth one download
instead of several.

## Everyday Display Housekeeping

Compared to bouncing between System Settings panes, betterdisplay for mac keeps the routine chores one click
away in the menu bar: change refresh rate and screen rotation, move displays around relative to each other,
designate any display as the main one, and simplify creating mirrored sets. It even improves zoom quality and
enables high-quality screenshots on 1080p panels. That breadth is why display manager mac tends to stay open
all day rather than being opened only when something breaks.
